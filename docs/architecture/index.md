# Architecture

How each subsystem is designed and what its v1 builds. The numbered docs are cited by section (integrity-engine.md §10.9), so their section numbers stay put.

* [Careers apply flow](apply-flow.md) - The candidate apply page: the notice before any collection, three steps that work without JavaScript, direct-to-storage resume uploads, the public submission endpoint, per-tenant branding stylesheets under the content security policy, tenant careers hosts, and the follow-up questionnaire page a signed link opens.
* [Employer branding](branding.md) - An organization's brand colour, second colour, heading font, logo, favicon and sharing image: the WCAG 2.2 AA check that refuses a failing colour with the nearest passing shade, brand image uploads straight to storage with their type, size and malware scan, how the careers pages serve them on their own origin, the header at the top of every email to candidates, and what the embeddable widget reuses.
* [Change history](change-history.md) - Who changed a hiring template, an email template, a job, a custom role or an organization setting, when and what: change records written by an EF Core interceptor in the same save, lines in plain words matched by element ids, Last updated on each read contract, the paged history routes, and how to track another kind.
* [Email to candidates](email.md) - Organization email templates in Liquid with a rich-text body over fixed variables, the email composer (a template or a blank email changed for one send, previewed with the candidate's details), each email kept as it went for Messages, sending through the worker, the rules that the rejection email goes only with Reject, the follow-up questions email only with SendQuestionnaire and the talent pool invitation only with Invite to apply, and what is not built (replies, an inbox).
* [Rich text](rich-text.md) - Job descriptions and email messages as one stored format, sanitized HTML limited to the editor toolbar's elements: the canonical form the server writes, Liquid inside an email's HTML, the plain-text part, the Tiptap editor with variable chips and its shortcuts, and the migration that converted plain text.
* [Integrity Engine: Applicant Authenticity & AI-Assistance Signals](integrity-engine.md) - The Authenticity and AI-assistance assessments: level rules, the collection timeline, the signal catalogue, the analyzers and the versioned IntegrityRuleset, retention, the LLM consistency check, content forensics, synthetic applicants and golden files, and the Signal Network design.
* [Job Distribution Engine: In-House One-Click Post & Teardown](job-distribution.md) - One-click publish and honest teardown: what teardown can mean per board, the posting states, worker reconciliation, the five XML feeds, the public job-board API, Google for Jobs JSON-LD and the Indexing API queue, the sandbox board, and the distribution settings.
* [MCP server for AI assistants](mcp-server.md) - The remote MCP server at /v1/mcp: Streamable HTTP in stateless mode, signed in with an organization API key and nothing else, eight tools on the hiring API's own services, candidate-written text marked as data, no integrity output, and the values any MCP client needs to connect.
* [Messaging and background work](messaging.md) - Wolverine on Postgres: publishing from an endpoint through the outbox, the worker queue, database roles and provisioning, how tenant messages keep row-level security, idempotency keys and [Deduplicated], retries and dead letters, cron schedules, and telemetry.
* [Real-time updates](realtime.md) - How an ITenantEvent reaches open dashboards: the API's own Wolverine cluster in wolverine_api, the SignalR hub at /v1/hubs/events and its tenant and job groups, the packages/realtime client and cache invalidation, the C# to Zod contract test, and scaling out.
* [Sign-in, organizations and tenant isolation](sign-in-and-tenancy.md) - Identity providers and email links, abuse controls, API key authentication, configured domains, organizations, permission checks, built-in and custom roles, team members and invitations, job access through each job's hiring team, and tenant isolation through named query filters and Postgres row-level security under screenhire_app, with Dev SSO and Mailpit locally.
* [Talent pool](talent-pool.md) - Keeping a strong candidate in mind: the application ends without a rejection and keeps everything, only with the candidate's own permission for future roles from the apply form, which runs out after the organization's period (12 months by default, 6 to 24); the Talent pool page, inviting someone to apply for another job or adding them to one, the signed link to stop being kept, the daily expiry sweep, job access, and what is not built.
* [Workflow Engine: Per-Job Hiring Workflows from User-Defined Templates](workflow-engine.md) - Hiring templates and per-job workflow definitions: stages, scorecards, follow-up questionnaires, automations with triggers, conditions and actions, the guardrail that integrity never rejects, the execution model, the builder, webhook subscriptions with signed deliveries, and what the v1 engine runs.
