# Design

The UI rules every screen follows (ADR-0010, amended by ADR-0013). The `design-review` agent reads section 2, the review checklist, on every run.

* [ScreenHire design system](design-system.md) - The one source of truth for every screen: principles, the review checklist the design-review agent enforces by ID, colour and status icons, type, layers, page templates, screen sizes, components, words, dates, icons, shortcuts, verification UI, employer branding, the marketing site's bands, type and rules, and what v0.3 changed on each screen.
* [Marketing site messaging](marketing-site.md) - What the marketing site sells and how it says it: who it is for, the five selling points as pain, promise and proof with their sourced numbers, the home hero, the voice and copy rules, the page map, the compare pages (a source and a date on every competitor claim), and what may be claimed and what is never said.
* [November 2026 redesign](redesign-2026-11.md) - Which mockup direction the owner picked for each candidate-page and setup surface, the design-system rule changes the owner approved, the audit's corrections that cut noise and dense text before anything is built, and the product decisions the Launch readiness tasks build on.
