Candidate page redesign

Directions for the six things you flagged in the preview walkthrough. Each surface has two or three directions, a one-line reason for each, and one marked Recommended. Pick one per surface. Where a direction changes a design system rule, a dashed label names the rule by its id, so the change can go into the design system before it ships.

Hard limits every direction keeps

  • Evidence, not verdicts. A level in words, its reasons, evidence, counter-evidence and a next step. No score and no percentage, anywhere.
  • No reject control in any verification view, and nothing pairs a verification filter with an end stage.
  • AI help is information. It never moves Clear, Review or Verify, and it never looks like a warning.
  • Nothing about emotion, intent, honesty or personality, and nothing that sets apart a candidate who used the assistive-technology setting.
  • Status is always its icon, its colour and a word.

  • RecommendedMy pick for that surface.

What changes

  • Authenticity and AI assistance become one Verification column, sorted Verify, then Review, Dismissed and Clear, with Pending last.
  • The filter row counts each state, so one look says two to verify. Under a verification filter, bulk moves still leave out the end stages.

Watch

  • Verify can read as Verified at a glance. The mark or the id-card icon and the tooltip carry the difference, and screen readers hear "Verify, verification suggested".
  • Dismissed is the one extra word. A reviewer set the level aside, so the row goes quiet but keeps a trace and a tooltip with who and why.
  • Review is also a task kind on My tasks. Surface 6 renames that task to Give your scorecard so one word means one thing.

Where the review log went

  • Mark explained, Undo and notes sit under the finding they concern. Explaining decides nothing: the level, filters and automations never read it.
  • Dismiss sits beside the level, because it sets the whole assessment aside. Once every finding is explained it reads Dismiss all, the same button.
  • Reads through an API key belong to no finding, so they are one caption on the audit line, with the full list in Activity.

States a group can be in

  • Found something (warning triangle, only for groups that fed the level), Information only (sparkles, AI help), Nothing found (quiet check), Turned off or Not counted (grey, from the organization's settings).
  • A candidate added by hand shows "Not checked" on Network and Form use, by source alone. Nothing on the page tells apart a candidate who used the assistive-technology setting.
  • The ruleset's summary names categories today ("network, identity and content checks"). It should name these six groups instead.
